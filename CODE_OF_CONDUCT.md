# Code of Conduct

This project is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

Examples of expected behavior include being considerate, giving and receiving constructive feedback respectfully, focusing on evidence and the work, and helping keep discussion welcoming and professional.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insults, discrimination, threats, deliberate intimidation, and publishing another person's private information. Do not post patient-level, identifiable, or controlled-access data in this repository, its issues, or pull requests.

Project maintainers may edit or remove content that violates this policy and may restrict participation when needed to protect the community. To report a concern, contact the maintainer through [GitHub profile](https://github.com/ekikeh). Please do not report sensitive personal or patient information in a public issue.

This policy is adapted from the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
